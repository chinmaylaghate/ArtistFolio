# ArtistFolio App Store Assets

## Design plan
- Brand: black and white "artist folio" wordmark, ultramarine #3B2BF0 accent, warm orange #E24E20 from the artwork.
- Font: Inter Display (close match to the logo).
- Screens: real app build screens from AppStore/Screenshots, placed in a simple iPhone frame.
- Copy: short and simple. One idea per image.

## Story order (previews and screenshots)
1. Discover: Where art finds its home (Home + Artwork detail)
2. Browse: Find art you love (Search + Home)
3. AR Preview: See it on your wall (View on Wall + Artwork detail)
4. Connect: Follow and contact artists (Artist profile + Home)
5. For Artists: Share your art. Keep 100%. (Studio + Profile)

Generator scripts are in _source.
